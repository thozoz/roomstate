# Enclosure Design

3D-printed two-part enclosure for RoomState (ESP32-C3 temperature/humidity monitor).
Modelled in Fusion 360 via the Fusion MCP server. This document tracks the design as it evolves;
sections marked **TBD** are filled in as components get measured.

## Print Target

- Printer: Bambu Lab P2S
- Material: PLA
- Goal: fast print, minimal plastic. No supports needed for either part.
- Clearance on all mating/seating surfaces: 0.15–0.2 mm

## Overall Structure

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Outer size | 93.2 × 58.2 × 25.6 mm |
| Inner cavity | 90 × 55 × 22 mm |
| Wall / floor | 1.6 mm (4 perimeters at 0.4 mm, no infill needed) |
| Lid | 2 mm plate + 1.5 mm inner lip, 0.2 mm clearance |
| Corners | outer R3, inner cavity R1.5, lid lip R1.3 |
| Fastening | 4 bosses, M2 self-tapping. Boss Ø5, pilot hole Ø1.6, lid through-hole Ø2.2 |
| Plastic | Base 22.2 cm³, Lid 10.4 cm³ (solid volume) |
| Orientation | Desk-clock style: screen faces up on the lid, USB-C and sensor grille on side walls |

Coordinate convention (used in all scripts): X = length, Y = width, Z = height. The inner cavity is fixed at X 2–92, Y 2–57, Z 2–24 and
every component position below is in these absolute coordinates; the outer shell is 1.6 mm outside that (0.4–93.6 / 0.4–58.6 / floor from 0.4). Units in Fusion API are cm; all scripts use a `MM = 0.1` helper.

The model is generated by Fusion API scripts (via the Fusion MCP server), not driven by user parameters. Changing a dimension means
editing the script values and regenerating the Base/Lid components; all component models (OLED, ESP32, DHT22 STEP files) stay in place.

| Component | Position (mm, absolute) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| DHT22 | PCB vertical at X 11.4–13, Y 22–50; sensor body Y 18–38 with mesh facing the X=0 wall | Own compartment, isolated from heat sources |
| Divider wall | X 14–15.2, full width, Z 2–22.3 (stops under the lid lip) | 8 × 6 mm cable notch at floor, Y 46–54 |
| ESP32-C3 SuperMini | On 3.5 mm rails, X 17–35, Y 2.5–25, USB-C toward the front wall | Headers pointing up |
| Rocker switch | Front wall, X 36–55.2 | Snap-in, body inside to Y 14 |
| OLED 0.96" | Mounted under the lid, board centre (45, 21), pins toward +Y | See OLED section |
| TP4056 USB-C | On 2 mm rails, X 64.5–91.5, Y 10–28 | Connector face recessed 1.1 mm inside the X=94 wall |
| 18650 in cut holder | Along X, holder X 16.5–91.5, Y 37–57 | Peg + rail + end stops, against the back wall |

Heat separation: TP4056 (charging heat) is ~55 mm from the DHT22, ESP32 ~16 mm, plus the divider.
Firmware runs WiFi/BLE off at 80 MHz so the ESP32 itself is negligible. Expect +1–2 °C drift on the
DHT22 while charging; mitigations under consideration: lid vents over TP4056, reading CHRG pin to
flag/offset readings, dropping charge current to 500 mA (R_PROG 1.2k → 2.4k).

## Cutouts and Features

### Base
- **DHT22 grille** (X=0 wall): 12 vertical slots, 0.9 mm wide, 1.9 mm pitch, Y 17–39, Z 3.2–17.3 — directly over the sensor mesh.
- **USB-C port, TP4056** (X=94 wall): 12.5 × 7.3 mm, Y 12.75–25.25, Z 3.7–11. Sized for the plug overmold (~12 × 6.5) since the receptacle face is recessed 1.1 mm.
- **USB-C port, ESP32 debug/programming** (Y=0 wall): 12.5 × 7.3 mm, X 19.75–32.25, Z 4.45–11.75. Same reasoning. Optional — can be left out of the print if not wanted.
- **Rocker switch cutout** (Y=0 wall): 19.2 × 13.2 mm, X 36–55.2, Z 6.4–19.6.
- **Board retention (snap-fit clips)**: vertical posts 1.2 mm thick outside the board edge, with a 0.6 × 0.8 mm hook overhanging the board top (+0.2 clearance). Press the board down, the post flexes, the hook holds it. No screws.
  - TP4056: 2 clips on each long edge (X 67–71 and 85–89, at Y 8.8 and 28), rear stop rib at X 63.3–64.5. Front is located by the USB-C in its cutout.
  - ESP32: long edges carry the headers, so clips sit on the rear edge (Y 25–26.2) at X 18–21 and 31–34, either side of the antenna. Side guides at X 15.9–17 (Y 3–24) and X 35–35.9 (Y 15–24, short to clear the rocker body). Front located by the USB-C.
- **Battery holder locating features**: Ø4.6 × 0.9 peg at (52.8, 47) (flush with the holder floor so the cell never touches it); rail X 18.3–87.3, Y 35.8–37, 5 mm tall; end stops X 14–15.3 (merged with the divider) and 90.3–92, 5 mm tall.
- **Screw bosses**: (6.2, 6.2), (87.8, 6.2), (6.2, 52.8), (87.8, 32). Three corners plus one mid-wall on the right (the corner spot is taken by the battery holder). Pilot hole 8 mm deep.

### Lid
- **OLED window**: 26 × 15.3 mm at X 32–58, Y 15.5–30.8 (4.2–19.5 mm below the pin edge). On this module the visible area starts right at the glass edge nearest the pins and runs 15 mm; the black flex zone is at the far end of the glass.
- **OLED bosses**: 4 × Ø3.8, 2 mm standoff, Ø1.5 pilot hole, at (33.5, 9.75), (56.5, 9.75), (33.5, 32.95), (56.5, 32.95).
- **Chimney vents** over DHT22 compartment: same slot pattern as the grille, X 4–12. Air enters via side grille, exits through lid.
- **Screw holes**: Ø2.2 through, at corner boss positions.

## Components

### OLED 0.96" SSD1306, 4-pin I2C, white — DONE (real STEP model)
Ruler measurements were inconsistent, so the component is now the community STEP model
`OLED_27x27.step` (KiCad library, github.com/louiscklaw/kicad_library), which matched every
measurement that could be trusted. Dimensions from the model, all "p" values measured from the pin edge:

| Dim | Value |
|---|---|
| Board | 27.3 × 27.3 mm, 1 mm thick |
| Mounting holes | 4 × Ø2.1 slots, centres 2.05 mm from the pin edge and from the far edge (Y spacing 23.2). In X the slots run 18.7–22.7 mm apart; **the actual board has round holes at the outer end**, so bosses sit 23.0 apart (first print had them at the slot middle, 20.7 — wrong, lid reprinted). |
| Glass | 27 × 19 × 1 mm, p = 4.15 … 23.15 |
| Visible / lit | visible area p ≈ 4.2 … 19.5 (starts at the pin-side glass edge, 15 mm long); flex/black zone p 19.5 … 23.15 |
| Pins | 4 × 2.54 header on the pin edge, pointing down (away from the glass), 9 mm long |

Placement: board centre X = 45, pin edge at Y = 35 (pins toward the battery side), board top at Z = 22, glass top at Z = 23 (1 mm below the lid underside).

### DHT22 breakout, 3-pin — DONE (measured + STEP sensor) — **sensor on hand is dead** (solder ball inside, reads 90–114 °C); replacement pending, same module type keeps the box unchanged. AHT20/SHT31 (I2C) would also fit and is the better sensor.
| Dim | Value |
|---|---|
| PCB | 28 × 13 × 1.6 mm, 3-pin header on one short edge |
| Sensor body | 15.1 × 20 × 7.7 mm (STEP `DHT22.stp`, TrailCurrent KiCad lib), plus 5.1 mm mounting tab at the far end → 25.2 total (measured 26) |
| Sensor position | body starts 12 mm from the pin edge, lies flat on the PCB, overhangs the far PCB edge by ~9 mm (total module length ~37) |
| Mesh area | ~20 × 14 mm on the face away from the PCB |

Placement: PCB stands vertical, parallel to the X=0 wall, at X 11.4–13, Y 22–50, Z 3.25–16.25.
Sensor body Y 18–38, Z 2.2–17.3, mesh face at X 3.7 (1.7 mm from the wall). Header at Y 50, pins toward +Y,
next to the cable notch. Module sits loose between wall and divider (2.7 mm play in X); no clips yet.

### TP4056 USB-C charger (with DW01A/8205A protection) — DONE (measured)
Two variants were available (27 × 18 and 19 × 15); the larger one is used — more copper, runs cooler, and there is room.

| Dim | Value |
|---|---|
| Board | 27 × 18 × 1.0 mm |
| USB-C | protrudes 1.4 mm past the board edge; total height board underside → connector top = 5 mm; receptacle ~9 × 3.3 |
| Pads | B+/B−/OUT+/OUT− on the edge opposite the USB-C |

Placement: board X 64.5–91.5, Y 10–28, on two 2 mm rails (Z 2–4) so the port cutout stays above the floor.
Board edge sits 0.5 mm back from the inner wall; USB-C face ends at X 92.9, i.e. 0.7 mm inside the outer surface (does not stick out).

### ESP32-C3 SuperMini — DONE (real STEP model)
STEP from github.com/mithro/esp32-to-433mhz (`esp32-c3-supermini.step`), matches the measured 18 × 22.5 board.

| Dim | Value |
|---|---|
| Board | 18 × 22.5 × 1.0 mm |
| USB-C | on a short edge, protrudes 1.5 mm, top at 4.2 mm above the board underside |
| Headers | 2 × 8 pins, 2.54 mm, along the long edges, **soldered pointing up** (decided). Pin stubs under the board ~2 mm → board sits on 3.5 mm rails. Pins top out at Z 11.6. |

Placement: board X 17–35, Y 2.5–25, board underside at Z 5.5 on two 3.5 mm rails at X 20–22 and 30–32 (inboard of the header rows). USB-C faces the front wall (Y=0),
face at Y 1.0 → 0.6 mm inside the outer surface (wall is now 1.6). (Moved 14 mm left from the first layout to make room for the rocker switch.) Pins used by firmware: GPIO7 (DHT data), GPIO0 (SDA), GPIO10 (SCL).

### 18650 Li-ion cell, 1200 mAh, in a cut-down spring holder — DONE (measured)
Cell Ø18 × 65 mm (the cell on hand is an 18650, not the 14500 first planned).
Runtime at ~35–40 mA (ESP32-C3 @ 80 MHz, WiFi off, OLED on, DHT22): ~30 h. Light-sleep between reads would stretch that to ~10 days — firmware task, later.

Holder: a 2×18650 spring holder cut down the middle to a single slot. One side keeps the holder's centre wall, the other side is open and rests against the box's back wall.

| Dim | Value |
|---|---|
| Holder (after cut) | 75 × 20 × 19 mm, ~1 mm floor |
| Floor hole | Ø5, centred (~38 mm from the end) |
| Solder tabs | at both ends — bend them inward/upward before fitting, there is no room past the holder ends |

Placement: X 15.3–90.3, Y 37–57 (open side to the back wall), Z 2–21. Held by: a Ø4.6 × 2.5 peg on the floor into the holder hole, a 1.2 mm rail along the inner side (Y 35.8–37, 5 mm tall), 5 mm end stops at X 15.3 and 91.5, and the back wall. No screws. Cell centre Y 47.5, Z 12; cell top Z 21.

Consequence: the back-right corner boss would collide with the holder, so it moved to (87.8, 32) — between the TP4056 and the holder. Lid hole pattern is asymmetric now.

### Power switch — DONE (KCD1 rocker, standard dims assumed)
Two options on hand: a KCD1-style rocker (~21 × 15 flange) and an SS12D00 mini slide. Rocker chosen: snap-in panel mount,
no screws or glue, works with a 2 mm wall. Rated 10 A, irrelevant at 40 mA.

| Dim | Value (standard KCD1-101, verify) |
|---|---|
| Flange | 21 × 15 mm, ~2 mm proud of the wall |
| Body behind flange | 19 × 13 mm, ~14 mm deep, then 2 pins ~6 mm |
| Panel cutout | 19.2 × 13.2 mm |

Location: front wall (Y=0), cutout X 36–55.2, Z 6.4–19.6 (centre X 45.6, Z 13). Body reaches inside to Y 14, pins to Y 20 — clear of the ESP32 (moved to X 17–35), TP4056 (X 64.5+) and the OLED (Z 21+).

## Wiring

Soldered connections (no Dupont — loosens inside a closed box). 24–26 AWG, 5–8 cm runs, heat-shrink on every joint.

| From | To |
|---|---|
| TP4056 OUT+ | rocker switch |
| rocker switch | ESP32 5V |
| TP4056 OUT− | ESP32 GND |
| TP4056 B+ / B− | holder tabs + / − |
| OLED VCC / GND / SDA / SCL | ESP32 3V3 / GND / GPIO0 / GPIO10 |
| DHT22 VCC / GND / DATA | ESP32 3V3 / GND / GPIO7 |

Note: TP4056 OUT is battery voltage (3.0–4.2 V) into the SuperMini 5V pin (regulator input). Works, but marginal at low charge.

**Backfeed / power switch:** the SuperMini's own USB-C connects VBUS straight to the 5V pin, so plugging the ESP32 USB while the battery is wired to the same pin would push 5 V back into TP4056 OUT+ and the cell. Solution chosen: a slide switch in series **TP4056 OUT+ → switch → ESP32 5V**. Switch off = battery fully disconnected from the ESP32, USB debug is safe, no diode needed. Charging via the TP4056 port works regardless of switch position (B+/B− path is not switched). Switch: KCD1 rocker on the front wall, right of the ESP32 USB-C.

## Print Notes

- Base: print as modelled (open side up). Overhangs: USB-C/rocker cutouts are ≤ 19 mm bridges, fine for PLA.
- Lid: `Lid.stl` is exported already flipped — outer face on the bed, bosses and lip up. The OLED window and vent slots print as holes.
- 4 perimeters / 0.4 nozzle give solid 1.6 mm walls; 3–4 top/bottom layers; infill irrelevant.
- M2 × 6 self-tapping screws for the lid (4×) and the OLED (4×).
